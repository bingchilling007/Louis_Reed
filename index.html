The Story Behind the Strategy

I Don't Just Advise
on Justice. I Lived It.

After serving 14 years in federal prison, I didn't retreat—I rebuilt. I became a primary architect of the First Step Act of 2018, the most significant criminal justice reform legislation in decades, impacting over 650,000 incarcerated Americans.

Today I lead strategy at the highest levels of policy, media, and philanthropy—advising corporations, producing documentaries, and writing stories that shift the culture around justice. My lived experience isn't a backstory. It's my greatest credential.
